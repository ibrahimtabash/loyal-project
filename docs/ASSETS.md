# Demo photography

Bundled fonts: IBM Plex Sans Arabic and DM Sans, downloaded from Google Fonts. Their SIL Open Font License notices are included in `public/fonts/`. The application serves font files locally and does not contact Google Fonts at runtime.

Local demo images were downloaded from Unsplash. They illustrate a fictional homewares store and are not a real product catalogue. Replace them with the merchant's actual product photographs before selling.
